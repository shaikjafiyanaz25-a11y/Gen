CAREPLUS MULTISPECIALITY HOSPITAL
DEPARTMENTS

Cardiology:
Deals with heart and cardiovascular-related medical care.

Dermatology:
Provides care related to skin, hair and nail conditions.

Orthopedics:
Provides services related to bones, joints and muscles.

Pediatrics:
Provides healthcare services for children.

Neurology:
Provides services related to the nervous system.

ENT:
Provides services related to ear, nose and throat.

Ophthalmology:
Provides eye-related healthcare services.

General Medicine:
Provides general medical consultation and primary healthcare services.
