CAREPLUS MULTISPECIALITY HOSPITAL
APPOINTMENT INFORMATION

Patients can request appointments using the Hospital FAQ and Appointment Helper.

For an appointment request, the user provides:

- Patient name
- Phone number
- Department
- Doctor
- Preferred date
- Preferred time

Patients should carry the following documents when visiting:

- Government identification
- Previous medical reports, if applicable
- Previous prescriptions, if applicable

Appointment requests are demonstration records for this academic project.

The application does not provide medical diagnosis or personalized treatment advice.
