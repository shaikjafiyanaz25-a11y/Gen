CAREPLUS MULTISPECIALITY HOSPITAL

CarePlus Multispeciality Hospital is a fictional hospital created for an academic RAG project.

Location:
Madanapalle, Andhra Pradesh, India

Hospital Working Hours:
The hospital is open 24 hours for emergency services.

General OPD:
Monday to Saturday
8:00 AM to 8:00 PM

Emergency Department:
Available 24 hours.

Hospital Services:
- General Medicine
- Cardiology
- Dermatology
- Orthopedics
- Pediatrics
- Neurology
- ENT
- Ophthalmology

Appointment:
Patients can request an appointment through the Hospital FAQ and Appointment Helper application.

Important:
This application provides general hospital and administrative information only. It does not provide medical diagnosis or personalized treatment advice.
